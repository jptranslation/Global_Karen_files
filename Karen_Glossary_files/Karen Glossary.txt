S'GAW KAREN–ENGLISH GLOSSARY

=====================================================
OLD TESTAMENT (Genesis–Malachi)
=====================================================

Source: all 23,145 Old Testament verses of the KJV, compared verse by verse with the S'gaw Karen Bible (Mason 1853, Sgaw_Karen_Bible_indexed.txt).
How to read an entry:  English = Karen  (also: other forms Mason uses)  [consistency]
Consistency counts the OT verses that contain the English word and shows how many of those also contain the Karen form or one of the listed forms. Example: [99%, 700/705] means 700 of the 705 verses that mention Moses contain the Karen spelling.
Main lists include only terms at 70% or higher and in at least 5 verses. Terms at 50–69% are listed separately as "preferred, but varies".
