## Spacing and punctuation rules (as used in the Karen text of Chapter 1)

These rules come from counting every space and punctuation mark in the chapter: 246 commas, 177 full stops and 434 spaces. Where the text breaks a rule, the place is listed under "Exceptions".

### Spacing
1. **No spaces between words.** Within a sentence the Karen runs together. A normal space appears only after punctuation, before a Bible reference's numbers, and inside the {MH} tag.
2. **One space after every comma and full stop.** All 246 commas are followed by a space (or a closing quote). Every full stop is followed by a space, a closing quote, or the end of the line.
3. **No space before a comma, full stop or closing quote.**
4. **No zero-width spaces (ZWSP).** Chapter 1 has none. Your instruction file (section 3) says to add ZWSP by script. So the plain text is the "before ZWSP" form.
5. **Paragraphs are separated by a blank line.** The English has no blank lines, and the Karen leaves out the English page numbers (18, 19, 20 …). It also joins lines that the English splits at page breaks.

### Punctuation marks
6. **Western comma and full stop (`,` `.`)** are used, never the Burmese-style ၊ or ။.
7. **Every sentence ends with a final particle followed by a full stop:**

| Sentence type | Final particle + `.` | Count in Ch. 1 |
|---|---|---|
| statement | လီၤ. | 108 |
| negative statement | ဘၣ်. (with တ … ဘၣ်) | 19 |
| "wh-" question (why, how, who) | လဲၣ်. | 7 |
| yes/no question | ဧါ. | 3 |
| command or request | တက့ၢ်. | 4 |
| completed action (in quotations) | လံ. | 2 |

8. **No `?` or `!`.** English questions and exclamations become a full stop after the right particle. For example, "What a busy life He led!" (24.1) ends with လီၤ., and "is not this the Christ?" (28.1) ends with ဘၣ်ဧါ.
9. **Commas are used much more often than in English.** The most common places are:
   - before ဒီး ("and") when it joins clauses or list items (about 106 times);
   - after each item in a "whether … or …" list: X မ့ၢ်ဂ့ၤ, Y မ့ၢ်ဂ့ၤ, (17.3, 20.3, 22.1, 25.3);
   - after a time or condition clause that ends in အခါ, ဖဲ …, or လၢ … အဂီၢ်;
   - after the speech verb, before a quotation: စံးဘၣ်အီၤ, “…”.
10. **Quotation marks are curly (“ ”).** The full stop goes inside the closing quote (`လီၤ.”`), and then there is a space before anything that follows.
11. **An English dash (—) becomes a hyphen with no spaces around it (`-`).** Examples are in 19.1, 22.1 and 26.3. A hyphen is also used for verse ranges (၄:၇-၁၄).
12. **Ellipsis** is written as spaced full stops (`. . .`), as in 27.1.

### Numbers, references and tags
13. **Bible references:** book name, then one space, then chapter:verse in Karen numerals, then a full stop. Use a comma and space between verses and a hyphen for a range. Examples: `မးသဲ ၈:၁၇.` `လူၤကၣ် ၂:၄၈, ၄၉.` `ယိၤဟၣ် ၄:၇-၁၄.` The English "Verses 29, 39" becomes `အဆၢဖိ ၂၉, ၃၉.`
14. **Chapter headings** use Karen numerals: `အဆၢဒိၣ် ၁` on its own line, with the title on the next line and no final punctuation. Section headings (ဒီပုၢ်ဝဲၢ်အတၢ်အဲၣ်, နီၢ်ကစၢ်တၢ်ဖံးတၢ်မၤ) also have no punctuation.
15. **{MH} tags** keep Arabic numerals and come after one space at the end of the paragraph: `…လီၤ. {MH 17.1}`. The Karen tags never have a space after `{`, but the English has `{ MH 17.1}`. Your instruction file says to copy the tag exactly, so decide which form you want.
16. **Poetry:** the English puts the Matthew 4:15-16 quotation on separate lines. The Karen joins it into one paragraph with commas.

### Exceptions to these rules in Chapter 1
- **19.4:** two spaces in `မးသဲ  ၄:၁၅, ၁၆.  {MH 19.4}` (both before the numbers and before the tag).
- **28.1:** two spaces before `{MH 28.1}`.
- **28.2:** no space before the tag (`လီၤ.”{MH 28.2}`).
- **20.2 and 25.2:** a stray space at the end of the line, after the tag.
- **21.1:** `“တၢ်ကွဲးအသးစံးဝဲ,” ”လံာ်…` has a closing quote (”) where an opening quote (“) should be. Also, `…ဒ်လဲၣ်”` has no full stop before the closing quote.
- **25.2:** the quote `“ပိာ်ယခံတက့ၢ်”` has no full stop (the sentence continues after it, so this may be intended).
- **Missing blank lines:** there is no blank line between these paragraphs: 20.2/21.1, 22.1/22.2, 25.1/25.2, 25.4/25.5, 26.2/26.3, 27.1/28.1.

### Note on the rest of the book
Chapters 1–4 and 20 onward follow the rules above. **Chapters 5–12 look different.** They have spaces between words, the old character ၥ instead of ာ, and the digit ၀ (zero) instead of the letter ဝ (about 100–230 times per chapter). Some chapter headings also add `း` after the number or start with "Chap. 12". Those chapters will need clean-up before they match Chapter 1.

## Spelling and alignment issues found in the Karen text

1. **Jesus** is spelled ယ့ၣ်ရျူး twice (MH 26.2 and 27.1). Everywhere else in the chapter (26 times) it is ယ့ၣ်ၡူး, which matches the Bible.
2. **Gentiles** is spelled ပှၤတခိဘီမုၢ်ဖိ in MH 20.3 (Karen tag 20.2). MH 25.4 and the Karen Bible (207 times) use တခီဘီမုၢ်.
3. **Tender / courteous** is spelled တၢ်သးကပုးလုး in MH 22.2 but တၢ်သးကပုးလုာ် in MH 24.1. Neither form is in the Karen Bible, so check which spelling you want.
4. **Reveal** is spelled လီၢ်ဖျါထီၣ် in MH 28.2. Elsewhere it is လီၣ်ဖျါ.
5. **Proclaim** is spelled စံၣ်တဲာ်တဲလီၤ in MH 27.1. The other 3 times it is စံၣ်တဲၤတဲလီၤ.
6. **Paragraph tags are off by one** around page 20. The Karen file puts the Matthew 4:15-16 poem inside {MH 19.4}, but the English tags it {MH 20.1}. So Karen {MH 20.1} is English 20.2, and Karen {MH 20.2} is English 20.3.
7. **Quote mark:** MH 21.1 has a closing quote (”) where an opening quote should be, before လံာ်တၢ်ကွဲးအသး.
8. **"Temple"** in MH 20.3 is translated တၢ်လုၢ်ဟံၣ် (outer court = တၢ်လုၢ်ဟံၣ်ဂၢၤလၢခိ). The Karen Bible normally uses a different word for the temple, so you may want to make this match.
