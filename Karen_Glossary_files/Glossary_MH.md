# Karen–English Glossary: The Ministry of Healing

**Sources:** MH_English_Edit_1.0.txt and MH_Karen_Edit_1.0.txt. The Karen file has chapters 1–12 and 17–22; chapters 13–16 and 23 onward have not been translated yet.

**Method:** The English and Karen were matched paragraph by paragraph, giving 780 matched pairs. Chapters 12–22 use the {MH} tags. Chapters 1–11 were matched by paragraph order and length, and checked against the Karen tags where they exist. Before counting, old-font characters in chapters 5–22 (ၥ, ၦ, ႇ, ၀, ၁်, ၇, ရှ) were converted to standard Unicode.

**Rule for inclusion:** a term is listed only if the Karen form appears in at least 3 of the paragraphs where the English word appears, and in at least 60% of them.

**Columns:** *Match* = the number of paragraphs with both the English word and the Karen form, out of the paragraphs with the English word. *%* = that as a percentage. A slash (/) means two forms, both used. Parentheses mean an optional part.

Chapter 1 details (spacing, punctuation and the chapter 1 typos) are in *Glossary_MH_Chapter_1.md*. Spacing and punctuation rules for all books are in *Karen_Punctuation_and_Spacing_Rules.md*.

## Notes

### Spellings that differ from the Karen Bible
1. **Babylon:** MH has ဘၤဘူလိၣ်. The Bible uses ဘၤဘူၤလိၣ် (297 times) and has ဘၤဘူလိၣ် only once.
2. **Decapolis:** MH has ဒ့းကးပလံၣ်. The Bible (Mark 5:20) has ဒ့းကၤပလံၣ်.
3. **Calvary:** MH uses both ကရၤနံၤ and ကရၤနံ. The Bible (Luke 23:33) has ကရၤနံ.
4. **Eve** (နီၢ်အံၣ်အု), **Jerusalem** (ယရူၤၡလ့ၣ်), **Capernaum**, **Nazareth**, **Peter**, **Paul**, **Moses**, **Philip** and the Bible book names all match the Bible.

### Old-font spellings found while counting
- **Chapters 5–22 write ၡ as ရှ.** For example, Jesus is ယ့ၣ်ရှူး and Isaiah is ယရှါယၤ. This is another old-font habit like ၥ and ႇ, and has been added to the spacing and punctuation rules. Chapter 1 also has two ယ့ၣ်ရျူး typos.

### One Karen word for several English words
Some Karen words cover several English words. When translating back from Karen, use the context to pick the English:
- ပှၤကညီ = man, men, human, humanity
- နီၢ်ခိ = body, physical
- တၢ်ကူတၢ်သိး = dress, clothing
- ပှၤတခီဘီမုၢ်ဖိ = stranger(s) and Gentile(s)
- တၢ်နါ = demons, devils, evil spirits
- လီၤလး = wonderful, wonder, miracle
- တၢ်မူ / တၢ်အိၣ်မူ = life. Use တၢ်မူ for life itself (life-giving, water of life) and တၢ်အိၣ်မူ for a way of living.
