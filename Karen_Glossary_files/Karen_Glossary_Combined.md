# S'gaw Karen–English Glossary (combined)

This glossary combines all the earlier glossaries into one:

- **Bible, Old Testament:** every KJV verse compared with the S'gaw Karen Bible (Mason 1853).
- **Bible, New Testament:** the same, for the New Testament. The Matthew 1 sample is included.
- **MH:** *The Ministry of Healing*, chapters 1–12 and 17–22 (780 matched paragraphs), plus terms found only in chapter 1.
- **STC:** your *Steps to Christ* Karen draft (273 paragraphs).

**Which spelling to use.** Where the spellings differ, use the Karen Bible's spelling. The column **Karen — use this** gives the Bible form whenever the Bible has the term. If the Bible doesn't have the term, it gives the MH form, or the STC form if only STC has it.

**The MH and STC columns:**
- **same** = the book uses the Bible form.
- **⚠ spelling differs** = the book spells it differently. Correct it to the form in the *use this* column.
- **(same word, other form)** = the same word with a different grammatical form, such as an added တၢ်, ပှၤ, အ or မၤ. This is fine.
- **(different word)** = the book uses another word or phrase, not just another spelling. This is a choice for you; it is not automatically wrong.
- **source** = this book is where the form in the *use this* column comes from.

**Numbers.** A count like 909/942 means 909 of the 942 verses (or paragraphs) that contain the English word also contain the Karen form. **varies** means the Bible's rendering changes with the context (50–69% consistent), so treat it as a preference, not a rule. *ch. 1* means the term was counted in MH chapter 1 only.

Karen text has zero-width spaces between syllables, following your translation instructions.

## 18. Notes on method

- **Bible:** the KJV and Karen Bible files were matched verse by verse (23,145 Old Testament and 7,957 New Testament verses). A term is listed if it reaches 70% consistency in at least 5 verses. Terms at 50–69% are marked *varies*. Names are included if they appear in at least 8 verses (OT) or 3 verses (NT).
- **MH:** 780 English and Karen paragraphs were matched. A term is listed if the Karen form appears in at least 3 of the paragraphs with the English word and at least 60% of them. Old-font characters were converted before counting.
- **STC:** the 273 tagged paragraphs of the draft were matched to the English. Terms at 50–69% are marked *varies*.
- **Merging:** entries from different sources were put on one row when they share an English word (for example *bless*, *blessed* and *blessing*). Where one English word has several meanings, each meaning is labelled in the row.
- **Corrections made while combining:** the earlier MH chapter 1 note said MH's word for "temple" (တၢ်​လုၢ်​ဟံၣ်) didn't match the Bible, but it does match the New Testament. Decapolis, Calvary and Eve were checked directly against the Karen Bible (Mark 5:20, Luke 23:33, Genesis 3:20).
